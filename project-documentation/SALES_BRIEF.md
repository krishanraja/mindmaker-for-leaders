## Product

CTRL is a calm AI decision partner for founders and small-team CEOs. Make Your Mind Up is the warm onboarding front door; CTRL is the product; `makeyourmindup.ai` is the canonical domain.

Short pitch: **A calm decision partner for the calls only you can make. CTRL tests one real decision against live evidence, carries one portable memory, and gives you a daily read that is actually yours.**

## Best-fit buyer

An AI-native founder or small-team CEO who already uses AI daily, has no time or staff to filter the field, and has an important decision in motion. See [`ICP.md`](./ICP.md).

## Lead angles

### The decision

“What is the expensive call you are carrying mostly in your head?”

CTRL reframes it into its AI-native version, checks its load-bearing claims, and separates evidence from judgement.

### The daily read

“Three minutes in your ear before the first meeting.”

Not a newsletter or RSS feed. The same ranked pool is filtered through what the leader is actually building and deciding.

### The memory

“Stop teaching every AI who you are from scratch.”

CTRL keeps context structured, correctable, owned, and portable.

### The blind spot

“One useful reflection, grounded in what you have actually shown.”

It stays tentative and is never saved as truth until the leader confirms it.

## Demonstration path

1. Start at `https://makeyourmindup.ai`.
2. Let the one-question-at-a-time flow capture the shape of a real problem.
3. Show First Lens.
4. Open Today and the briefing control.
5. Weigh one real decision.
6. Show the evidence trail and the point where judgement remains with the leader.

Avoid a feature tour. The product should be understood through one useful outcome.

## Pricing

- Free forever: Memory, context export, Blind Spot, daily personalised briefing, three weighs per month.
- Edge Pro, $49/month: unlimited weighs, multi-model cross-examination, decision watch, artifacts, drafting, live MCP pull, artifact email delivery.

Do not offer retired one-time diagnostic SKUs or quote legacy subscription prices.

## Objections

### “I already use ChatGPT or Claude.”

Good. CTRL is the context and judgement layer around those tools. It remembers the business, narrows the AI world, and checks the calls that generic chat cannot know are important.

### “Is it another dashboard?”

No. The minimal version sends the useful part by email and audio. The app exists when the leader wants to inspect, decide, correct, or talk back.

### “Does it make decisions for me?”

No. It improves the evidence and the frame, then makes the remaining judgement visible.

### “How private is it?”

Context is user-scoped, correctable, exportable, and deletable. Do not claim certifications not evidenced in the product. Never say customer context trains a public model.

### “Is this consulting?”

No. CTRL is software. Mindmaker's advisory business is retired.

## Qualification

Strong fit: daily AI use, real decision, small team, high value on judgement, low tolerance for setup.

Weak fit: generic chat request, enterprise collaboration suite, implementation tooling, or consulting procurement.

## Copy guardrails

One concrete outcome per message. Plain language. No em dashes, fake urgency, invented statistics, or fabricated cohorts. Use `makeyourmindup.ai` in every CTA.
