# CTRL user outcomes

Status: Historical

> **Historical commercial reference, not current guidance.** Current outcome language and claim boundaries live in [`../docs/current/commercial.md`](../docs/current/commercial.md).

Last reconciled: 2026-08-10

These are intended product outcomes, not automatically measured claims. Quote observed product behavior unless a metric has a production definition and result.

## First session

- The leader feels understood without completing a profile project.
- CTRL turns the onboarding answers into one recognizable lens and one useful first call.
- The leader sees that the product will reduce noise rather than create another inbox.

## First week

- The leader receives a daily read or listen that is materially narrower than a news feed.
- At least one real decision is decomposed into what checks out, what is shaky, and what remains judgement.
- Corrections and reactions visibly improve what CTRL carries forward.
- The leader can use value without learning the whole app or returning to it every day.

## Ongoing

- Context becomes more current, structured, and portable.
- Repeated delivery does not duplicate subscriptions, handoffs, emails, audio, interests, or confirmed blind spots.
- Decision watch and new evidence show when a prior assumption weakens.
- Blind Spot becomes a constructive reflection loop rather than an assessment score.
- The leader spends less attention filtering AI changes and more attention on the calls only they can make.

## Product health outcomes

- One ranked pool powers Today, briefing, and no-login delivery.
- Every promoted insight retains provenance.
- The leader can understand why an item appeared.
- Thin or missing data produces a calm fallback or one easy question, never fabricated certainty.
- Primary journeys work at desktop, mobile, and 320px without clipped meaning or horizontal overflow.

## North Star

The measured flywheel is context in and judgement out in the same week: a leader holds a real brain with at least five current facts and weighs at least one decision. See [`NORTH_STAR.md`](./NORTH_STAR.md).
