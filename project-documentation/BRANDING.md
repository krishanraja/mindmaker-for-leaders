## Brand architecture

- Product: CTRL.
- Domain and public front door: Make Your Mind Up at `makeyourmindup.ai`.
- Builder and legal operator: Mindmaker.
- Retired hostname: `ctrl.themindmaker.ai`, redirected to the canonical domain.

Use CTRL in product copy. Use Make Your Mind Up only for the warm onboarding experience or domain. Do not present them as separate products.

### Onboarding brand handoff

The public intake is the emotional bridge into CTRL, not a separate brand reveal.

- It begins with the warm Make Your Mind Up pink-orange mark and near-black surface.
- Each completed question moves the mark, accent, controls, and background one calibrated step toward CTRL.
- The thinking and result states use the canonical Mindmaker CTRL icon, deep instrument surface, and emerald `#00D9B6` accent exactly.
- The transition communicates growing usefulness, never a score or setup-completion demand.
- Reduced-motion preferences remove decorative animation without removing the current visual state.

Do not swap the icon abruptly, retain the Make Your Mind Up palette on the result, or introduce a second wordmark.

## Promise

Tagline: **A quieter way through AI.**

Positioning frame: **Build the AI-native version of your business.**

One line: CTRL is a calm AI decision partner that filters the AI world through what a leader is actually deciding, checks one real call against live evidence, keeps portable memory, and sends a short daily read or listen.

## Voice

CTRL sounds like an intelligent person who is genuinely curious to help the leader uncover more.

- Warm, direct, calm, specific.
- Confident about evidence, tentative about interpretation.
- First person when CTRL is speaking.
- Short sentences and plain words.
- No corporate throat-clearing, hype, FOMO, emojis, or em dashes.
- Never says it has decided for the user. The judgement stays with the leader.
- Never turns thin signal into certainty. Ask one useful question instead.

Good: “I think there may be a pattern here. Want to test it?”

Bad: “Our comprehensive AI-powered analysis recommends immediate action.”

## Visual system

- Dark, premium, instrument-like, never generic SaaS cards or a text RSS wall.
- Emerald accent `#00D9B6`, used with restraint for focus and state.
- Premium category motifs make the curation system legible at a glance.
- Segoe UI Variable Display for headlines and expressive questions.
- Segoe UI Variable Text for controls and body copy.
- System mono only for compact evidence metadata.
- Minimum 44px signature controls. No clipped text, awkward wrapping, or horizontal overflow.
- Navigation uses one restrained rounded-stroke icon family, not blank circles.

## Interaction language

- One primary ask at a time.
- Recognition before recall.
- Useful defaults and one-click confirmation.
- Settings is always reachable.
- Learning prompts explain why the answer helps, make the action reversible, and disappear after one choice.
- Audio is a signature control with clear play, pause, progress, and talk-back states.

## Vocabulary

Prefer: Today, Decide, Blind spot, Memory, briefing, weigh, anchored to, worth a look, what changed, one thing to check.

Avoid in user copy: orchestration layer, intelligence engine, harness, embedding, pipeline, RAG, schema, agentic workflow, diagnostic surface.
