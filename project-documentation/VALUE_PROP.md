## Binding promise

CTRL gives an overwhelmed founder a quieter way through AI: the small number of changes worth carrying, a better-tested decision, and context that compounds instead of disappearing into another chat.

## The two taxes it removes

### Zero-context tax

Every AI tool starts from zero, so the leader repeats who they are, what the business is doing, and what matters. CTRL keeps one structured, correctable, portable memory.

### Evidence tax

Important calls get made on gut and a half-read feed because nobody has time to check what the call is resting on. CTRL decomposes the call, verifies load-bearing claims, and shows what remains judgement.

## Value by moment

- First visit: a warm intake and First Lens that proves CTRL heard the shape of the problem.
- Every morning: a short, visual read of what changed and why it matters here.
- When a decision lands: a sourced pressure test and one clear breakpoint.
- When context changes: a small correction or one-click learning prompt, not a setup project.
- When the leader is away: the same useful intelligence by email and audio.
- Over time: better ranking, better questions, better memory, and an evidence trail of how judgement changed.

## Why it is different

- One product and one brain across onboarding, Today, briefing, Decide, Blind Spot, Memory, and delivery.
- Control Center curation enters the same pool; there is no duplicate feed.
- Personalization explains the anchor that earned an item its place.
- Blind Spot is tentative and consented, never an automated personality judgement.
- Audio can be talked back to and answers from the current briefing.
- The app can disappear while the value continues through no-login delivery.

## Pricing value

Free is a real daily product: Memory, context export, Blind Spot, the personalised briefing, and three weighs per month.

Edge Pro at $49 per month is for leaders who use weighing as an operating habit: unlimited weighs, cross-examination, decision watch, artifacts, drafting, live MCP pull, and artifact email delivery.

## Proof rule

Use product mechanics and measured system behavior as proof. Never quote aspirational percentages, fake cohorts, or unsupported time savings as results.
