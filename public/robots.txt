# CTRL (ctrl.themindmaker.ai)

# Posture: the PUBLIC surfaces are deliberately open to search AND to AI
# crawlers (answer-engine optimisation). GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot
# keep their explicit Allow: that is intentional, do not remove it.
#
# What is closed: the authenticated app routes (src/router.tsx, everything
# under AuthedLayoutRoute + RequireAuth) plus /think (redirects into the authed
# dashboard) and /preview (an unlinked dev/QC fixture harness). They render no
# public content, they only bounce a crawler to the login screen, and they
# should never appear in an index or an AI answer.
#
